Privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

email-manager-mcp is used only by its owner, for his own Google accounts. No one else can sign in to it.

  • What it accesses: Gmail messages, labels and drafts, using the gmail.modify permission, so the owner can search, read, send, label and move his own mail to Trash.
  • Where it runs: only on the owner's computer, when the owner asks for it. Login tokens are stored in Windows Credential Manager on that computer.
  • Who sees the data: the owner, and the AI assistant he uses in his own session (Claude, by Anthropic) when he asks it to work with his mail. The app has no server of its own and does not sell, share or advertise with any data.
  • How to revoke: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
